Privacy Policy
Last Updated: August 8, 2026

◆ Welcome to Puzzle Empire

Welcome to Puzzle Empire, a mobile puzzle game designed for entertainment and gameplay.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Puzzle Empire and how third-party advertising services may handle information.

Puzzle Empire does not require users to create an account or log in to play the game.

◆ Information We Collect

Puzzle Empire does not ask users to provide personal information such as their name, phone number, home address, or password in order to play the game.

The game may store gameplay-related information locally on the user's device, such as:

  • Game progress
  • Completed levels
  • Coins, gems, and stars
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Other gameplay-related information

This information is used to provide the game's features and maintain gameplay progress.

◆ Advertising — Google AdMob

Puzzle Empire uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the game.

Depending on the implementation and applicable settings, advertisements may include:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • Rewarded advertisements

Google AdMob may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and information related to advertising interactions for purposes such as delivering, measuring, and improving advertisements.

The type of advertising shown may depend on the user's device settings, consent choices, applicable laws, and Google's advertising systems.

For more information about Google's handling of information, please review Google's privacy documentation.

◆ Rewarded Advertisements

Puzzle Empire may offer optional rewarded advertisements.

If a player chooses to watch a rewarded advertisement, the game may provide an in-game reward after the advertisement has been successfully completed.

Players are not required to watch rewarded advertisements in order to use the basic gameplay features of the application, except where a particular optional game feature specifically requires a rewarded advertisement.

◆ Internet Connection

Puzzle Empire may require an internet connection for certain features, including loading advertisements and other online-dependent functionality.

Advertisement availability is not guaranteed. An advertisement may occasionally be unavailable because of network conditions, advertising inventory, geographic availability, or other technical reasons.

◆ Third-Party Services

Puzzle Empire uses third-party services that may process technical, device, or advertising-related information.

The primary third-party advertising service currently used by the application is Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the applicable policies of those services for additional information.

◆ Children's Privacy

Puzzle Empire does not knowingly request personal information from children through the game.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to us through the application in a manner that should not have occurred, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

◆ Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

◆ Your Choices

You may uninstall Puzzle Empire from your Android device at any time.

You may also manage certain advertising and privacy-related settings through your Android device and applicable Google advertising or consent settings.

Game information stored locally on your device may be removed when application data is cleared or the application is uninstalled, depending on your device and Android settings.

◆ Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Puzzle Empire, applicable requirements, or the services used by the application.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last Updated" date will be changed accordingly.
